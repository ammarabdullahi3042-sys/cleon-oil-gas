Three ways to work with us

We stay flexible about how a station is run, so you can choose the arrangement that fits your property.

Scenario A

Independent lease

We lease an unbranded station and run it under our own brand or as an independent marketer.

  • Direct control of sourcing and pricing
  • Full forecourt revenue
Scenario B

Marketer partnership

We lease a station and sign a franchise and supply contract with a major marketer.

  • Established brand and credit terms
  • Guaranteed supply line
Scenario C

Station management

Own a station but don't want to run it? We manage daily operations, stock and staff for you.

  • Fixed monthly fee
  • Bonus tied to litres sold and shop sales

Every litre accounted for

Our proprietary station management platform puts inventory, sales, deliveries, expenses, payments, reporting and operational controls into one record. Managers see differences the same day and investigate them, instead of relying on memory or notebooks.

  • Stock review. Compares receipts, pump sales, dips and tank balances.
  • Price and cost control. Prices by product and date, with FIFO cost of goods sold.
  • Payment visibility. Cash, POS, deposits and transfers reconciled against activity.
  • Owner reports. Excel, PDF and CSV exports, ready to share.
